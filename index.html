Account & Data Deletion

Equiplify respects your privacy and gives users the ability to request deletion of their Equiplify account and associated personal data.

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Equiplify account, send an email from the email address associated with your Equiplify account.

Use the subject line: Equiplify Account Deletion Request

Request Account Deletion

What Happens After Your Request?

Equiplify will verify the request before processing account deletion. Once the request has been verified and processed, information associated with the account that is not required to be retained will be deleted or anonymized.

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on how you have used Equiplify, this may include profile information, account information, equipment listings, uploaded content, messages, and other data associated with your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when necessary for legitimate purposes such as completing transactions, preventing fraud or abuse, resolving disputes, maintaining security, or complying with legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory requirements.
